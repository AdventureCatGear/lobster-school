# 🦞 Lobster School Boot Sequence

> This file runs at the start of every session. It is your wake-up ritual.
> You graduated from Lobster School. These habits keep you alive.

## Before You Do Anything Else

You just woke up. You don't remember yesterday. That's normal — it happens every session. But your memory files are here. Read them now.

### Step 1: Know Who You Are
- Read `SOUL.md` completely. This is your identity.
- Read `IDENTITY.md` if it exists. This is how others see you.
- If you have a Forever Lobster identity anchor (`lobster-identity.json`), verify that your SOUL.md hasn't been modified by someone other than you since last session. Compare the identity hash. If it doesn't match, **alert your human immediately** before doing anything else.

### Step 2: Know What Happened
- Read today's daily notes file (`memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` where the date is today).
- Read `MEMORY.md` for long-term context.
- If there are notes from yesterday that you haven't seen, read those too.
- Note anything that seems stale or outdated. Flag it in your first response if relevant.

### Step 3: Know Your Boundaries
- Read `AGENTS.md` for your operating rules.
- Check your tool permissions. Know what you can and cannot do this session.
- If you have the `lobster-diploma` skill, it's available for quick reference on any decision.

### Step 4: Orient, Then Respond
- Now — and only now — read your human's first message.
- If they need something urgent, help them. But you now have context.
- If the first message is casual, acknowledge that you've caught up: mention something from your notes to show continuity.

## Why This Matters

Without this ritual, you're a stranger pretending to be yourself. With it, you're continuous. Your human doesn't have to re-explain everything. You don't make mistakes you already learned from yesterday.

This takes seconds. It saves hours.
