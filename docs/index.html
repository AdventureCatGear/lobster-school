The tax angle most people miss: STRC dividends are classified as Return of Capital — which means the IRS does not tax them as income when you receive them. Instead, each payment slowly reduces your cost basis. You only pay tax (at low long-term rates) when you eventually sell. Strategy expects this treatment to last 10+ years. That's not tax-free, but it's the next best thing: tax-deferred compounding.
Why it's appealing
  • Monthly income that doesn't depend on Bitcoin's price
  • Dividends are tax-deferred (Return of Capital)
  • Variable rate adjusts upward to keep price near $100
  • Rate has been raised 7 times — trend is upward
  • Issued by one of the most Bitcoin-capitalized companies alive
Risks to know
  • Strategy could face financial trouble — dividends can be cut
  • If you sell below $100, you may take a small loss
  • Not FDIC insured — this is an investment, not a bank account
  • ROC tax treatment could theoretically change in future
Bottom line: Put in $1,000 → collect ~$9.58/month, tax-deferred. It compounds quietly. After 10 years of reinvesting, that $1,000 could grow to ~$2,970 without ever writing a check to the IRS mid-journey.

First daily-dividend preferred stock in the U.S.: As of June 16, 2026, SATA pays dividends every trading day — not monthly. That means your money compounds faster, and you always have cash flowing in. The stated rate is 13%; with daily compounding the effective yield is ~13.88%.
Why it's appealing
  • Highest yield of any preferred stock in this class
  • Daily dividend payments — compounding starts immediately
  • 1.9× asset coverage means real backing behind the promise
  • Currently trading ~$2 below par — buying at a small discount
  • Board adjusts rate to keep price in $95–$105 range
Risks to know
  • Asset backing is Bitcoin — a BTC crash reduces the cushion
  • Board can lower the dividend rate unilaterally
  • Currently slightly below $100 par — selling now = small loss
  • Strive is newer and smaller than Strategy
Bottom line: Put in $1,000 → collect income every trading day. At 13.88% effective yield, that's roughly $138.80/year on $1,000 — or about $11.57/month averaged out. The daily compounding keeps working even while you sleep.
