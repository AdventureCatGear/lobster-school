How the system works

Saylor invented a concept he calls "Digital Credit" — structured income backed by Bitcoin as the underlying capital. Here's the cycle in plain English:

🏦Investors buy STRC shares for ~$100 each
→
₿Strategy uses that cash to buy more Bitcoin
→
📈BTC on the balance sheet appreciates over time
→
💸Strategy pays your dividend from cash reserves and BTC gains
→
🔄Rate adjusts monthly to keep the share price near $100

The variable rate is the stability mechanism. Each month, Strategy looks at where STRC is trading: if it drifts below $100, they raise the dividend to attract more buyers and push the price back up. If it trades above $100, they can dial the rate back slightly. The dividend absorbs the volatility so the price doesn't have to swing wildly.

Important to know: STRC is not technically secured against Strategy's Bitcoin — there's no direct lien on the coins. What backs it is Strategy's overall balance sheet, their ability to keep issuing shares and buying BTC, and their $900M+ in cash reserves. In practice, 843,738 BTC is an enormous cushion — but it's worth understanding the structure honestly.

The tax treatment most people overlook

STRC dividends are classified as Return of Capital (ROC) by the IRS — which means you do not owe income tax when you receive them. Each payment instead reduces your cost basis in the shares. Tax is only triggered when you sell, and at that point it's at long-term capital gains rates (typically 15–20%) rather than ordinary income rates (up to 37%). Strategy has confirmed 100% of 2025 distributions were ROC, and expects this treatment to continue for 10 years or more. That's tax-deferred compounding — the same structural advantage as a Roth IRA, but available in a brokerage account. Full tax explainer →

How the system works

Strive's model is straightforward: accumulate as much Bitcoin as possible using capital raised through SATA, then pay SATA holders income from that growing BTC treasury. CEO Matt Cole describes it as "engineered Bitcoin-backed yield" — income you can receive without holding BTC directly or riding its volatility.

💼Investors buy SATA shares at ~$100
→
₿Strive uses proceeds to buy Bitcoin (debt-free)
→
📊BTC treasury grows; assets cover obligations 1.9×
→
📬You receive a cash payment every trading day
→
🔄Board adjusts rate monthly to hold price at $95–$105

The daily compounding is meaningful: a 13% stated annual rate paid every trading day (roughly 250 days/year) compounds to an effective yield of ~13.88%. That's real money — and it starts working from day one. Strive also intentionally stays unprofitable — every dollar of income gets reinvested into more Bitcoin, growing the treasury that backs your dividends.

Debt-free matters: Strive financed all 15,009 of their Bitcoin entirely through preferred equity — no debt. That means no creditors with priority claims ahead of SATA holders. You are first in line.
